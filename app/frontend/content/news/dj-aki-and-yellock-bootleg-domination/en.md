DJ AKi has been experimenting with harder styles lately, and for this one he teamed up with YELLOCK to bootleg Holy Priest & Rekkt's "Domination" into a full-force drum'n'bass banger. The track keeps the original's heavyweight pressure while pushing the tempo and grit into DnB territory, and it is available as a free download ahead of the 06S 25th Anniversary.
