Welcome to the official Follow The Bass website.

After living in Tokyo for over eight years, our founder, **Stefan**, is sharing his story. The injustice he encountered while working behind the scenes to support Japan's bass music scene became the driving force behind **Follow The Bass**, a new event brand built around drum and bass, dubstep and trap.

We are not here simply to make heavy bass sound loud. We want to create a place where artists, staff and audiences all respect one another — where **Honor** and a genuine bass music experience can exist side by side.

Why start a new brand now? Stefan speaks about the injustice behind that decision and the conviction he has poured into Follow The Bass.

For more on Stefan's profile and work, see his [artist page](/en/artists/stefan).

---

## Interview: founder Stefan reveals the truth behind the brand

### Q1: First, could you tell us about your background as the founder and how you came to launch Follow The Bass?

**Stefan:**
Hello everyone. I'm Stefan, founder of Follow The Bass.

For many years I worked behind the scenes rather than on stage — booking international artists, planning events and arranging funding — helping to develop Japan's bass music scene.

The biggest reason I started Follow The Bass is to restore the "Honor" and genuine respect for artists that the scene is losing. After experiences in which my passion and investment were unfairly taken from me and I was erased as if I had never existed, I decided to rebuild a healthy, proud community from scratch — one without lies or betrayal — with my own hands.

---

### Q2: You say it was "unfairly taken." What exactly happened?

**Stefan:**
It concerns the launch of the Tokyo-based event brand "Beginning" (Instagram: `@beginningtokyo`).

If you scroll all the way back to the very beginning of their Instagram account, the names of notable artists credited early on include **Siren** and [**Far Too Loud**](/en/shows/far-too-loud). But there is a truth the public does not know. The person who contacted those international artists directly, negotiated their appearances, and paid their fees out of his own pocket to make those bookings happen was none other than me, Stefan.

The reason the Beginning brand got off the ground and became known in the scene was the artist lineup I provided with my own money and passion. But once the events began to gain momentum, the organizers refused to acknowledge my contribution at all — not even a word of thanks — and betrayed me by pushing me out as if I had never existed.

---

### Q3: Did you not speak directly with the organizer, Sako?

**Stefan:**
I could not accept it, and I tried many times to talk to him directly, as a professional. But Sako's response was far too insincere to be acceptable.

When I pressed him, the moment he was cornered he insisted that he "doesn't understand English" and refused dialogue entirely. On top of that, he grew agitated, showed his irritation, and eventually shoved me away by force before fleeing.

That attitude — not only running from contracts and conversation in business, but trying to solve things by force — left me deeply disappointed. Stealing someone else's achievements and using force to push them away when things become inconvenient — there was not a trace of "Honor" or pride in any of it.

---

### Q4: After such a harsh experience, what musical direction and principles will Follow The Bass operate under?

**Stefan:**
Sonically, Follow The Bass will focus on drum and bass, dubstep and trap, pursuing heavy bass of overwhelming quality.

And the core value at the heart of the brand is "Honor."

The music scene rests on mutual trust between the artists who create great tracks, the staff who build the events, and the audience who supports the space. A brand that tramples on someone's achievements or tells insincere lies behind the scenes cannot create a party that truly moves people.

Follow The Bass makes the following three commitments absolute:

1. **Complete transparency and respect**: We will fairly recognize the contribution of every artist, staff member and partner involved, and treat them with sincere respect.
2. **A commitment to integrity (Honor)**: We will take responsibility for our words and actions, and we will never run away — we will always engage in sincere dialogue, whatever the situation.
3. **A genuine heavy bass space**: For everyone who loves drum and bass, dubstep and trap, we will provide a pure, top-tier sound experience free of vested interests and ego.

---

### Q5: Finally, a message to the fans and partners who will support Follow The Bass going forward.

**Stefan:**
The betrayals and injustice of the past were painful, but they also became a powerful motivation that made me realize what an event truly should be. We will build the best possible place where we can present music and culture with our heads held high — not a brand that quietly piles up insincere behavior behind the scenes.

To everyone who loves heavy bass and values truth and respect: please look forward to what Follow The Bass does next.
