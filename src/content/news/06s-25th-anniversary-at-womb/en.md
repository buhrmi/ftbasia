06S marks its 25th anniversary with an event at WOMB on Friday, November 6, 2026. The flyer lists Friction as the headline guest, alongside DJ AKi, YELLOCK, KEITA and YASUKI, with YUUKi MC hosting.

Additional artists are listed across WOMB's 1F and 4F floors. The flyer also advertises a live stream on [DJ AKi's Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/djaki_06s).
