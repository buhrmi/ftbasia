Bass2Face teams up with Exodus Records for **Thursday Clash**, a UK takeover at BRIX Club in Corner Village, Da Nang, on Thursday, November 5. The event runs from 10 pm to 6 am and is billed across drum & bass, UK garage, dubstep and jungle.

The flyer puts **CHAMBA** at the top of the lineup, billing him from the UK and crediting Exodus Records and Yamatai Records. Chamba has released music through Yamatai; in a [UKF label spotlight](https://ukf.com/read/label-spotlight-yamatai/), Yamatai owner James Williams names him among the artists representing the label's liquid drum & bass sound. [Beatport's Chamba catalog](https://www.beatport.com/artist/chamba/244208) includes releases on Yamatai Records.

The lineup also features KARNI and FRIENDS IN THE DANCE from Exodus Records, EZOR of Dispatch Recordings, JAKART of Sofa Sound Bristol, IVAX and RED.

The ticket listing gives prices of **100,000 VND presale** and **200,000 VND at the door**. [Get tickets through Likdo](https://likdo.asia/event/3082/).
