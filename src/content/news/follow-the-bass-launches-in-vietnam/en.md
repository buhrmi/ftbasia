Follow The Bass is preparing for its next chapter: bringing international bass music artists to **Da Nang, Vietnam**.

We are looking for local venues interested in hosting drum and bass, dubstep and other bass music nights. Our goal is to work closely with venue partners to create events that fit their space and connect Da Nang audiences with artists from around the world.

## A partnership designed to keep venue costs low

Follow The Bass intends to take responsibility for the costs and logistics of bringing international artists to Da Nang. That includes **flights and accommodation**, as well as coordinating the **necessary visa and other legal paperwork** for the artists.

We plan to recoup these event costs through ticket sales. Venues will not be asked to pay for artist travel or lodging, guarantee those costs, or cover a shortfall if ticket revenue does not meet our expenses. The financial risk for those costs stays with Follow The Bass.

We want venue partners to be able to focus on what they do best: welcoming audiences and delivering a great experience in their space.

## Looking for the right venue

We are keen to hear from Da Nang venues with an interest in electronic music and a welcoming atmosphere. If you are an owner or manager and would like to explore hosting a Follow The Bass night, let's talk about your space, the event format and what could work for your audience.
