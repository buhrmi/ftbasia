What if AI helped build the tool, not the track? Gridline is a free, open-source digital audio workstation built around a channel rack, step sequencer, piano roll, playlist and mixer. Its GitHub project credits Claude among its contributors, while the music-making stays in your hands.

The familiar workflow is backed by 178 built-in instruments and effects, including a seven-band Parametric EQ and Lattice, Gridline's six-operator FM synth. When you open an FL Studio project, Sytrus is mapped to Lattice, while other supported instruments and effects are mapped to their Gridline counterparts. Gridline can import FL Studio projects, zipped loop packages and presets, and export projects back to FL Studio format. These are Gridline's own implementations, not FL Studio itself, so individual features and sounds are not necessarily one-to-one matches.

The project is written in C++17, runs as a plugin inside other DAWs, and offers downloads for Windows, macOS, Linux and BSD.

[Explore Gridline on GitHub](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline) or [download the latest release](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/releases/latest).
